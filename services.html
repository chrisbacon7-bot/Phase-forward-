The questions I ask first

I diagnose before I prescribe.

Before we build anything, I pressure-test five things. The answers tell us what to build, what to cut, and what to leave alone.

  1. What's the real why?

    Most teams can't say it in one sentence. That's the first problem. Vision sells. If the why is fuzzy, everything downstream drifts.

  2. Where's the reach gap?

    Who are you not reaching, and why? Students, young adults, a groups ministry, or the campus 35 miles out. The gap is the strategy.

  3. Where do systems duplicate?

    Duplicated effort shows up in one ministry and across campuses. Three versions of the same form. Per-campus overhead. Redundant volunteer pipelines. Centralize where it scales. Keep local what's contextual.

  4. Where is ownership unclear?

    What must align across the team? What can align but the local leader calls? What does the local leader fully own? Blurry lines stall everything.

  5. Will it scale beyond you?

    If it only works because you work, it's not a system. It's a bottleneck. If your people can't run it when you're not in the room, we rebuild it until they can.

The C.L.E.A.R. method

Analyze, Construct, Unleash is how an engagement runs. C.L.E.A.R. is how I help leaders make decisions inside that work. In consulting I help a team work through hard calls in Analyze and Construct. In coaching I walk one leader through a decision.

CClarify the real problem
LLocate the tension
EExamine the options
AAct with clarity
RReview and refine

We open with how you're doing, not just how the ministry is doing. We review the commitment you made last time. Then we clarify the real problem, locate the tension, and examine the options. FAQ

Questions I get first.

How is this different from a typical church consultant?

I've spent 20+ years inside the local church doing the work. I diagnose before I prescribe, build what's simple enough to use Monday, and coach your leaders until they run it without me. I evaluate programs and systems, not just numbers, and I stay with your people through the change.

What does “Analyze. Construct. Unleash.” look like in real life?

Analyze is a few weeks of evaluating your programs, systems, structure, and numbers, plus conversations with your leaders. You get a one-page diagnosis. Construct is building the fix with your team. Unleash is 90-day coaching cycles until your team owns it.

Is this just about numbers?

No. Numbers matter. Leading indicators like retention, first-time guests, and volunteers tell you where you're headed. But data is only as good as the story you interpret. I spend as much time with your leaders as I do with your spreadsheets.

What does a typical engagement cost?

Are you available while you're still a campus pastor?

Do you work with non-church organizations?

Yes. Mission-driven nonprofits and faith-rooted organizations. The lean is ministry, but the principles travel.

Do you travel?

For speaking, yes, across the U.S. For consulting, most work is virtual with one or two onsite visits per engagement.
