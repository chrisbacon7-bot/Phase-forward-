The method

How I work: Analyze, Construct, Unleash.

Most leaders don't need more information. They need to see clearly, build simply, and lead well. That's the whole method. Three steps. No binders.

01
Analyze

See what's really happening.

I evaluate your programs, systems, staff structure, and the numbers. I look at the leading indicators: retention, first-time guests, and volunteers. Attendance is the lag measure. Numbers show where to look. Conversations explain why. Data is only as good as the story you interpret.

You get: a one-page diagnosis. What's working, what's broken, what's duplicated, and what to stop.

02
Construct

Subtract first. Then build what repeats.

I subtract first. We take out what's duplicated or heavy before we add anything. Then I build simple, repeatable tools and processes your team can run again next month.

You get: something your team can use Monday.

03
Unleash

Hand it to your leaders.

I coach your leaders, hand off ownership, and build on progress in 90-day cycles. We review what moved, keep what works, and take the next step until your team runs it on their own.

You get: a ministry that scales beyond any single strong leader.
